Attendance Tracker Web App

Points on this dashboard are drawn from attendance records logged in the Attendance Tracker Web App. If you're looking for that tool:

https://imresidentdashboardapp.pages.dev/

If you are a first-time user, instructions to sign up are:

60-Second Version

  1. 1 Phone browser → imresidentdashboardapp.pages.dev
  2. 2 Your Duke Email → wait for checkbox → Send
  3. 3 Check your spam folder for “Your sign-in link”
  4. 4 Click or copy the link, paste into Safari/Chrome on your phone, within 45 min
  5. 5 Confirm sign-in → Scan QR Code → Allow camera → point at Welcome QR

How to Check In (Long Version)

Step 0

Use your phone, not a laptop

Open Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). The check-in needs your camera, and the sign-in cookie “sticks” to whatever browser you use (so you don't have to continually log in). You can also bookmark this as an “app” on your iPhone.

Step 1

Go to the site

https://imresidentdashboardapp.pages.dev

Web app — nothing to download.

Step 2

Enter your Duke email

  • Use your full Duke email address.
  • For security, the site will not tell you that you're missing from the roster. It gives everyone the same friendly message on purpose.

Step 3

Wait for the security checkbox

A small Cloudflare widget appears under the email box. “Send sign-in link” stays greyed out until it finishes. If the button didn't light up, refresh the page and wait 5 seconds.

Step 4

Tap “Send sign-in link”

After the widget runs, you should see: “Check your email for a sign-in link.”

Step 5

Check your spam folder

I suspect this is where most errors are happening — Outlook is rude. The email is:

  • From: noreply@nicholasbrazeau.com
  • Subject: Your sign-in link

When you find it, hit “Not Junk” / “Report not spam” first. That teaches the filter, so if you need to sign in again in the future, the next one will land in your inbox.

Step 6

Open the link within 45 minutes

The link expires 45 minutes after it's sent and is single-use (i.e. you will have to start over at Step 1 if you wait more than 45 minutes or it is an old email).

Step 7

Open the link on the same phone you'll scan with

  • You are signed in with the web app on your device.
  • Tap “Confirm sign-in”.

Step 8

Confirm you're actually signed in

You should be back on the main page reading “Signed in as [Your Name].” There is now a button that says Scan QR Code.
